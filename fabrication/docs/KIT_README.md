## E. Assembly sequence

1. **Laminate the 3 base plies (PVA, weight, edges flush)** — SI001, SI002, SI003
2. **Glue GF slab / plinth onto engraved footprint on base** — SI004
3. **Pool boxes, floor ribs and pool floors on GF slab** — PO001×2, PO002×2, PO003, PO004×2, PO005×2, PO006×2, PO007×2, PO008, PO009×2
4. **GF bearing ribs, GF walls and GF glazing frames** — ST001, ST002, ST003, ST004, EN001, EN002, GL001, GL002, GL003 (optional acetate: GL005, GL006, GL007)
5. **Continuous walls: spine (beam slots), N wall, hall W/E, E skin, slants, garden, wing S wall, N-bar bulkheads** — ST005, ST006, ST007, EN003, EN004, EN005, EN006, EN007, EN008, EN009, EN010, EN011 (optional acetate: GL008, GL009, GL010, GL011)
6. **Drop in L1 plate (pool deck + wing + solarium)** — FL001
7. **L1 elements: wing ribs, west-zone face, notch wall, solarium parapet bands, cantilever box** — ST008, ST009, ST010, EN012×2, EN013, EN014×2, EN015, EN016, DE001, DE002, DE003
8. **Bleacher stacks (4 x 2 plies) against spine** — CI001×2, CI002×2, CI003×2, CI004×2
9. **L2 plate (terrace + south block floor)** — FL002
10. **South block: S, E and leaning N walls (bevel), hidden ribs** — ST011, ST012, EN017×2, EN018×2, EN019×2 (optional acetate: GL012)
11. **Hall structure: 6 inclined legs into L1 slots, 6 roof beams into spine slots** — ST013×12, ST014×12
12. **Curtain-wall frame against leg feet (+ optional acetate)** — GL004 (optional acetate: GL013)
13. **Sunshade fins into slots, S-B7 top beam on fin tops** — ST015×2, ST016, ST017×2, ST018, ST019, ST020
14. **Roofs RF-02, RF-03, RF-06, E strips; NE box walls + roof** — RF001, RF002, RF003, RF004, RF005, DE004, DE005, DE006
15. **Hall roof RF-01 (2 plies) + fascia: REMOVABLE, do not glue** — RF006×2, RF007

Glue: PVA wood glue for chipboard; tacky glue (no solvent) for acetate. Clean edges with a #11 blade. Engraved lines on plates mark where the next part stands; part tags are engraved on hidden faces.
