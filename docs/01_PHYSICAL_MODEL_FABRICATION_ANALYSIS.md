## 16. Potential fabrication problems

1. **Sub-ply walls.** 0.24 m walls scale to 1.25 mm, under one ply. They are fabricated at 1.59 mm (+27 %). East skin and NE box are affected.
2. **Long thin parts.** The north wall is 309 × 76 mm at 1 ply (warps), and S-B7 is 278 × 15.5 mm (fragile). Add 1–2 hidden cross-walls/bulkheads, and glue S-B7 down onto fin tops in one pass.
3. **Inclined legs (2.1 × 3.1 × 44.6 mm).** At 1 ply they snap at the corners. Proposal: laminate 2 plies (3.18 mm in-plane width kept as 0.60 m → 3.1 mm).
4. **Tapered beams (3.9 → 5.9 mm deep, 111 mm long).** Proposal: 2 laminated plies (3.18 mm vs 2.6 mm real, +22 %). The sloped top edge must match the RF-01 slope (0.2059).
5. **Sloped hall roof.** One flat plate at true length 117.7 mm, which needs a bevel or a hairline gap at the N bar and the S fascia. Use the fascia to hide the joint.
6. **Leaning south-block N face.** Cut as a flat plate at true length; both ends are trapezoidal. End walls take the lean profile.
7. **Curved walls.** Light well 2.05 (≈29 × 22 mm oval), curved wall 1.04 and the oval drum guard. Chipboard cracks at tight radii, so use **scored kerf-bend strips** (vertical scores on the hidden face) or stacked horizontal rings.
8. **Stairs:** riser below 1 ply (see §8).
9. **Glazing in an all-chipboard model** reads as opaque or empty unless a clear sheet is allowed (Q3).
10. **Chipboard thickness variance** (real stock 1.4–1.8 mm). Every slot and stacked height must reference `T_MAT`, never a hard-coded 1.5875.
11. **Laminated heights accumulate error.** The L1 deck at +4.00 = 20.8 mm = 13.1 plies. Wall heights are cut to exact scaled heights, not ply counts, and slabs sit on walls.
12. **Charring / smoke.** Long parallel cuts close together (CW frame bays) need ≥ 1/8" bridges, or should be cut as separate parts.
13. **Base flatness.** A single 16 × 13" chipboard ply will warp. It needs a laminate or a rigid substrate.
14. **Hall-roof data conflict** (§20.4) affects the beam profile by up to 3.6 mm at scale.

## 17. Proposed joint strategy

Keep it simple: glued butt joints, with location coming from engraving. Slots only where a part stands on a slab, tabs only where alignment is hard.

| Joint | Where | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Butt + engraved guide line** (default) | Walls on slabs, slabs on walls | Engrave wall footprints (FAB-GUIDES) on the slab's top face; wall sits on the line, wood glue (PVA) |
| **Through-slot, 1 ply** | Sunshade fins into L2 terrace & solarium slab; legs into L1 deck | Slot width = `T_MAT + FIT − KERF` (default FIT = 0, press-fit; parameterised); tab depth = slab thickness |
| **Notch / saddle** | Beam bearing on N-bar wall and leg tops | Rectangular notch in beam end, `T_MAT` deep |
| **Lamination with registration holes** | Base plies, beams, legs, roof RF-01 | 2–3 Ø 1/8" holes (cut) aligned with toothpicks/pins |
| **Corner butt with 1-ply overlap rule** | Wall-to-wall corners | Long wall runs full; short wall is shortened by `T_MAT` (computed parametrically) |
| **Kerf-bend score** | Curved walls, oval guard | Parallel score lines (SCORE layer, never CUT) on hidden face |
| **Locating tabs (2–3 per part)** | GF slab to base, CW frame to legs | 3 × `T_MAT` tabs into matching cut-outs |

No finger joints, no interlocking lap grids, nothing smaller than 3 mm in any engagement.

**Kerf rule set (to implement in Phase 4):**

- outer profiles offset **outward** by `KERF/2`;
- inner openings offset **inward** by `KERF/2`;
- slots sized to `T_MAT + FIT` after compensation;
- engrave and score geometry are **never** offset.

The uncompensated "design" curves stay on FAB-* layers. The compensated curves are generated onto the sheet CUT layer, so changing `KERF` only regenerates sheets.

## 18. Proposed part numbering system

The prompt's examples clash: S001 is used for both SITE and STRUCT. I propose unique 2-letter prefixes:

| Kit | Schedule ID | Engraved tag (≤ 5 chars, 1.5 mm single-stroke text) |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Site / base | SITE-001 | SI001 |
| 02 Primary structure | STRUCT-001 | ST001 |
| 03 Floors / slabs | FLOOR-001 | FL001 |
| 04 Roof | ROOF-001 | RF001 |
| 05 Exterior envelope (walls) | ENV-001 | EN001 |
| 05b Glazing / CW | GLAZ-001 | GL001 |
| 06 Circulation | CIRC-001 | CI001 |
| 07 Pool | POOL-001 | PO001 |
| 08 Details | DETAIL-001 | DE001 |

- Repeated identical parts get one number plus an instance suffix, for example STRUCT-002 ×6 tagged ST002a…f, so their grid position (C1…C6) is still traceable.
- Tags are engraved on the underside, hidden face or tab of each part, never on a visible face.
- Parts under about 6 mm get no tag; they are identified on the sheet by an adjacent engraved tag on the sheet scrap.
- Each part also carries user strings for `part_no`, `kit`, `sheet`, `source_guid` (the original object's GUID) and `exaggeration`.
